Project Site Name: Sparta Development Testing

Why I Chose this Name:

I choose this name as a refrence to one of my favorite video game families, the family works to help out those that need it and I believe it would be a good name for a family business of game testers.

Purpose:

The purpose of the site is to provide a video game testing site where developers can send in their games for help with bug testing, and or to become a bug tester themselves.

Scenarios:

Question 1: I am having problems with X part of my game, can this help me find the issue in this part?

Question 2: I am great at finding bugs in games, can this place help me put that to use in helping others?

Question 3: Can i trust this site to not steal my work to make as their own?

Color Scheme and Font Type:

The colors I will in this project are: #131515 for header text color, #2b2c28 as the color for the body, #339989 as the header/footer color, #7de2d1 as a second background color or a background color of modals, and #fffafb as the color for h1-h6 elements

My chosen fonts will be BBH Bogle for the header and footer and DM Sans for the body
